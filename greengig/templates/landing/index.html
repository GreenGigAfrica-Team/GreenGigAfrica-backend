Your work is verified.
Your pay is guaranteed.

Trust is GreenGig Africa's foundation. Every task, every submission, every payment is protected by layers of verification.

📸

Multi-stage photo proof

Photos required at task start, during, and completion. GPS location and timestamp captured automatically — no staging possible.

🤖

AI image validation

Every submission is checked by AI to confirm it contains relevant content — waste, trees, or task materials. Built on TACO and DeepForest datasets.

🏛️

Organisation approval before payment

Payments only release after a verified organisation reviews proof of work. Full transparency at every step.

📍

GPS location tagging

Device location is captured at the moment of every photo upload — confirming the worker was physically at the task site.
